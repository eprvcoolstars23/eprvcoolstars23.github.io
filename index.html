The Topic

Stellar variability phenomena, such as supergranulation and magnetically active regions, produce radial velocity signals that can easily impede the detection of Earth-like planets.

In this splinter we will discuss the current theoretical understand of these phenomena, the state of the art in modeling their impact on radial velocities. In particular, we will focus on the connecrion between convection and magnetism.

Splinter Format

The splinter session will be divided in two blocks, separated by a 30-minute cofee break:

  • Block 1: Stellar variability and its impact on extremely precise radial velocities
  • Block 2: Connecting convection and magnetic activity
Each block will include an invited talk, 3 contributes talks, and a 30-minute discussion.
We encourage the community to submit abstract for contributed talks, and to submit questions and inputs for the discussion session, to ensure an engaging and fruitful discussion.
