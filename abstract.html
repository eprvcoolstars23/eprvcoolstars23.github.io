Abstract Selection

Abstract will be selected based on their quality and relevance to the splinter topic by the organisers. Speaker selection will be made by 30 March 2026, and speakers will be notified by email.

In order to foster an inclusive and diverse environment, we will strive to select a diverse set of speakers in terms of career stage and representation. We encourage early career researchers to submit their work. We also will encourage a balance between theory and observation-based talks.

We however note that Cool Stars 23 follows a one-person-one-talk policy across the entire meeting. We still encourage you to submit your abstracts for the splinter and to contact us if selected for a plenary contribution.
